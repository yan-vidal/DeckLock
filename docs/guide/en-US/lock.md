# Unlocking

Type your account's password and confirm.

## Typing the password

- Use a physical keyboard, or touch the keys of the on-screen keyboard.
- The keyboard icon inside the field shows or hides the on-screen keyboard. Esc also hides it.
- The eye at the start of the field shows or hides what you typed.

With the Steam Deck controller and the on-screen keyboard open:

- Each trackpad points at the keys on its half of the keyboard. The trigger on the same side, or pressing the trackpad, types that key.
- The left grip is Shift and the right grip is Alt. LB erases and RB inserts a space.
- B, X or Y hide the keyboard. The keyboard icon in the field brings it back.

## Confirming

Press Enter, the A button on the controller or the arrow beside the field. If the password is wrong, the screen says so and you can try again. Many wrong attempts in a row can lock the account for a few minutes, depending on how the system is set up.

## Rest

After a while without use, the screen rests and shows only the background and the clock. Any key, touch or movement brings the password back.

## Top-right corner

Switch user, suspend, hibernate, restart and shut down. On devices with a battery, its charge level appears just above those buttons.

## This help

F1 or the ? button opens this help; Esc or the × closes it. The full guide is in DeckLock's settings.
