# Signing in

Choose your account, type your password and confirm.

## Account and session

If there is more than one account, switch with the arrows beside the picture, or with the ← and → keys while the password field is empty. The session, the desktop that will open, is in the selector at the bottom of the screen. The next sign-in starts with the account and session of the last one.

## Typing the password

- Use a physical keyboard, or touch the keys of the on-screen keyboard.
- The keyboard icon inside the field shows or hides the on-screen keyboard. Esc also hides it.
- The eye at the start of the field shows or hides what you typed.

If the Steam Deck controller is enabled for sign-in:

- Each trackpad points at the keys on its half of the keyboard. The trigger on the same side, or pressing the trackpad, types that key.
- The left grip is Shift and the right grip is Alt. LB erases and RB inserts a space.
- B, X or Y hide the keyboard. While it is hidden, the right trackpad moves the pointer and the right trigger clicks. To bring the keyboard back, touch the keyboard icon in the field.

## Confirming

Press Enter, the A button on the controller or the arrow beside the field. If the password is wrong, the screen says so and you can try again. Many wrong attempts in a row can lock the account for a few minutes, depending on how the system is set up.

## Power

Suspend, hibernate, restart and shut down are in the top-right corner. On devices with a battery, its charge level appears just above those buttons.

## This help

F1 or the ? button opens this help; Esc or the × closes it.
